TeamBuddy Account Deletion

To permanently delete your TeamBuddy account and all associated personal data (check-ins, photos, location history, notes, etc.), please send a deletion request to the developer.

What we need from you:

  • The email address you used to register
  • A short confirmation that you want to delete the account

Request Account Deletion by Email

We will process your request within 30 days and confirm deletion by email.
